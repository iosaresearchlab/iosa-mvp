2. What the VPI is, and what it is not

The Viral Performance Index (VPI) is our own measurement. It divides a video’s public view count by the median view count of the channel’s recent Shorts, so that a small channel and a large one can be compared on the same scale. It is a research output, not a certification, an accreditation, an award or an endorsement issued by any authority, and it carries no official status of any kind. Figures come from public platform APIs and may be incomplete, delayed or revised.
