2. What the VPI is, and what it is not

The Viral Performance Index (VPI) is our own measurement. It divides a video’s public view count by the median view count of that channel’s recent Shorts, so that a small channel and a large one can be compared on the same scale. It is a research output. It is not a certification, an accreditation, an award, an audit or an endorsement issued by any authority, and it carries no official status of any kind.

Figures come from public platform APIs and may be incomplete, delayed or revised. The service is provided “as is”, without warranty of accuracy or availability, and we accept no liability for decisions taken on the basis of these figures.
