IOSAResearch Lab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what data IOSA Research Lab processes, why, and how you can ask us to stop. It covers both the creators whose public metrics we measure and the people who order a commemorative item.

1. Who we are

IOSA Research Lab (“we”) is an independent research project studying how short-form video performs relative to each channel’s own historical baseline. The project has no profit purpose; optional commemorative items fund servers, API quota and development.

Data controller: TO COMPLETE: legal name, registered address, and VAT or tax number if applicable. Contact: iosa.research.lab@gmail.com.

2. Creator data

Our systems read publicly available metrics through the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Research API. For each measured video we store:

  • the channel name and public handle;
  • the video title, its public URL and its publication date;
  • public view counts, the computed baseline and the resulting VPI value;
  • the country and category under which the video was sampled.

We do not collect email addresses, contact details, private messages, demographic data or any non-public information about creators. We do not buy, sell, license or share this data with data brokers or advertisers.

3. Why we may process creator data, and on what basis

Some of this information can identify a person, so under the GDPR it is personal data even though it is public. We process it for statistical and research purposes, on the basis of our legitimate interest in publishing an open measurement of public content performance (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), with the safeguards for research set out in Art. 89 GDPR. Because we receive the data from the platforms rather than from the creator, Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR applies: informing every creator individually would require disproportionate effort, so we publish this notice instead.

4. How long we keep it

A measured video stays visible in the public index for 15 days from the moment we detect it. After that it is marked as expired and is no longer shown. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify a single video or channel may be kept longer for research continuity.

5. Creator rights and opt-out

If you are a creator, you can ask us to stop processing your data or to delete your records at any time, with no justification required. Write to optout@iosaresearch.com from any address, naming the channel. We remove the records and exclude the channel from future scans. You also have the right to access, rectification, restriction and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

6. Data of people who order an item

If you order a commemorative item we process your name, email address and shipping address, for the sole purpose of producing and delivering that item and meeting our accounting obligations. Payment card details never reach our servers: they are handled by Stripe.
